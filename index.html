Where we come from

Built by people who actually work in ServiceNow.

We're not career instructors who learned a platform to teach it — we're ServiceNow professionals who work inside the platform daily, across ITSM, ITOM, and platform administration. We started this course because we kept meeting talented people, students and career-changers alike, who wanted to break into ServiceNow but couldn't find training grounded in how the work actually happens on the job.

Everything in this course comes from real implementation work: the tickets we've closed, the workflows we've built, the mistakes we've made and fixed. Our goal is simple — get you to a place where you could sit down in a ServiceNow admin or developer seat on day one and not feel lost.

What you'll learn

A curriculum built around real tickets, not slides.

Each module mirrors a category of work you'd actually be assigned in a ServiceNow role — so what you practice is what you'll do.

01

ServiceNow Fundamentals

Navigate the platform, understand tables and the core architecture behind every ServiceNow instance.

02

ITSM in Practice

Incident, problem, and change management workflows — configured and run the way real service desks operate.

03

System Administration

Users, roles, ACLs, business rules, and UI policies — the day-to-day toolkit of a ServiceNow admin.

04

App & Workflow Development

Flow Designer, custom applications, and scripting basics to automate real business processes end to end.

05

Certification Readiness

Structured prep toward ServiceNow's CSA (Certified System Administrator) exam, with practice scenarios.

06

Capstone Project

Build and present a working ServiceNow application solving a real business problem, reviewed by your instructors.
